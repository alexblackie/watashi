In the last couple years the phrase "elbows up" has made rounds as a defiant cry
against the American imperialist infiltration of Canadian culture and economics.
While it is, unfortunately, rather cringe, it represents an important shift
towards domestic resilience against becoming a vassal state of an abusive former
ally.

However, the mindset it engenders won't help anyone. The sentiment is strong but
it's not a sentiment that will lead to constructive and lasting change.

We need to be "heads down", not just "elbows up".

Canada can only slingshot itself out of the US gravity well by itself gaining
enough economic and societal momentum to overcome the pull. Complaining online
about a rage-bait headline you read is not going to help Canada move forward.

Canada's strengths already give us every tool we need: we have personal liberty,
mostly-free markets, world-class educational institutions, land, water, energy,
and even a social security net to soften the personal risks of entrepreneurship.

There is no excuse. It's not Trump's fault. It's not Trudeau's fault. It's your
fault. It's my fault. It's OUR fault for not taking advantage of our superior
position to become the superpower we could be.

**All we have to do is build.** Start a company, found a non-profit, design a
prototype, foster a community... Do something productive to build the future you
want, don't just complain that the future you want doesn't exist.

---

I recently made a change to the website for my company's product,
[Worktree](https://about.worktree.ca). I changed the headline on the homepage,
and this was the commit message (the internal note I left about the change):

> _Make hero copy more constructive_
>
> _The old copy got the point across but was more destructive and cynical.
> We should try to be a bit more inspirational instead of harbouring
> resentment over the status quo._

This mindset shift is one that I think a lot of Canadians need to take to heart.

It's incredibly easy to fall into the trap of, "yeah fuck those guys!", but that
sort of cynical mindset is not one that builds the future of a nation and a
civilization.

You know how we can "fuck those guys"? We can out-build and out-perform them at
every level. We have the people, resources, and systems at our disposal to do
so. So let's get heads-down and do it. The only limit is yourself and your
mindset.
